## Summary
- **18 entries: 5 Blocking, 9 Adjust, 4 Cosmetic.**
- **RWBEM is human-bound in its *target*, not its *discipline*.**
  - What it researches is real human places. What it produces is location-catalog items (buildings, rituals,
    roles, tools, sub-spaces). How it asks is "does this place produce its own version?" All three are human,
    and all three block use for L1/L2 work.
  - Its research discipline is not human-bound and transfers almost word for word: LAW 0-R, chasing threads,
    NO FORCED FIT, one subject on its own terms, the Step D tier order, the Step F log, and "memory is not the
    method."
- **The GPS law has a direct biological analog:**
  - *The human management regime of a studied animal population is a coordinate, never a cause.* Who bred it,
    where it was kept, and how it was introduced do not make a trait.
  - The law's "other half" (the sequencing unlock) also maps: once the developer rules on the base population
    (Q-4 / L0), that population's domestication history becomes admissible. "Composition names the stock"
    becomes "the base ruling names the stock."
- **Under D-2, RWBEM proper has nothing to do for these races until Phase 7.** No locations means no picks. Its
  only near-term relevance is M-5's real star-system research, which needs a scope ruling against D-2 (Notes
  item 1).
- **EBEM is RWBEM with three changes:**
  - **Step B's inventory:** behaviors, signals, social structure, life history, and senses, instead of
    buildings, rituals, roles, and tools.
  - **Step C's question:** "does the resolved physiology keep the mechanism?", not "does this place have its own
    version?"
  - **Step F's log:** adds fields for the identity of each study population.
- **Biological examples in "Breaks for" are research prompts, not facts.** Verify them through M-2 under LAW 0-R
  and rule 5.

## Generalizes cleanly
These carry over into EBEM essentially verbatim, with only the nouns renamed.
- **All of LAW 0-R (RWBEM:5–82):**
  - "A RECALLED fact is not input data."
  - "A WRONG fact is worse than a MISSING one" (*a plausible number doesn't invite suspicion*). This matters
    even more for animal facts, which everyone half-remembers.
  - "One search against a pick establishes that the pick exists."
  - The seven "fully" items. Items 2 and 3 get renamed (HAR-RWBEM-08, -09).
  - No time limit and no search budget.
  - The "change a finding, or ornament one?" check.
- **Chasing threads (RWBEM:231–239)**, including logging threads that were noticed but not chased.
- **"When in doubt… OMIT it entirely, not… reframe it" (RWBEM:145–146)** and **"the fix is silence, not a
  disclaimer" (RWBEM:133–135).**
- **The "neither extreme" table (RWBEM:180–184).** Too little: refusing all animal material, which gives
  "humans with ears." Too much: transplanting animal behavior intact, which gives "animals in clothes." The
  operation: the resolved mechanism is the starting stock, and culture diverges from it.
- **NO FORCED FIT and ONE LOCATION ON ITS OWN TERMS (RWBEM:203–222),** with "location" replaced by "race"
  (`MASTER_PLAN.md` §8). This includes the relation test: delete every other race's name, and if the claim
  survives, it was relation.
- **"A blocked check is not an absence" (RWBEM:284–286).** In EBEM this becomes: an M-1 *Unresolved* tag is not a
  negative.
- **Step D's ordering principle (RWBEM:303–333):**
  - Canon outranks vision, vision outranks the current derivation, and the derivation outranks derived notes.
  - "A synthesis that predates the chain cannot validate the chain's own output."
  - Check the reconciliation docket before trusting a derived source.
- **All of Step F (RWBEM:345–370):** one log per subject, appended and never rewritten, exact search strings,
  fact-to-finding table, withheld vs. omitted, open threads. Also "a research log records attributes, never
  conclusions," so it stays admissible to a later cold run.
- **Lessons 2 and 4 (RWBEM:412–417)** and **"Working from memory looks like the method and is not the method"
  (RWBEM:394).**

## Carry-over into M-2 (EBEM)
**Taken step by step through RWBEM's own structure.** "Inherit verbatim" means copy the rule and rename nouns
only. "Change" means the rule's content must be rewritten.

### Step 0 — Governing laws (stated in full in the EBEM file, never cross-referenced; RWBEM:7 gives the reason)
- **Inherit verbatim:**
  - LAW 0-R (RWBEM:5–82).
  - NO FORCED FIT (RWBEM:207–214). "This race does not keep the mechanism" is a complete and legitimate answer.
    "Not resolved yet" must never read as "not possible."
  - ONE SUBJECT ON ITS OWN TERMS (RWBEM:216–222). Never use another race's L1 as a control or an implied normal.
- **The prohibition — change the subject, keep the shape.** Take it from the ULM copy's universe-agnostic
  wording ("a real-world basis is a coordinate, never a cause"), not from the original's station-nation
  wording. The EBEM form:
  - *A study population's human context (who bred or kept it, where, which program, how it was introduced) is
    a coordinate, never a cause of a race trait.*
  - Swap test, adapted from RWBEM:123–124: if a sentence's meaning would change when you swap the study
    population for another population of the same taxon that keeps the same mechanism, it describes the
    population's circumstances, not the mechanism.
- **The "other half" — inherit the structure as a sequencing rule:**

  | Stage | Admissible |
  |---|---|
  | **Before** Q-4 / L0 names the base population | Mechanisms only. Husbandry and domestication history are coordinates |
  | **After** the base is ruled, *and* M-4 says the creators used that population | That population's domestication history and selected traits are legitimately in scope, because they are causal through L0 |

  *"Composition names the stock" becomes "the base ruling names the stock."*
- **Inherit verbatim:** the named-site trap, re-aimed at folk meaning (HAR-RWBEM-04); "silence, not
  disclaimer"; "omit, don't reframe."
- **New, with no RWBEM counterpart, from the ULM copy's "what a site never hands down" rule:** *mechanisms can
  cross the engineering boundary (as M-1 resolves them); an animal population's* learned *traditions cannot,
  because no animal raised the race.* This holds unless L0 says otherwise. This line separates Animal-Inherited
  Universals (M-3) from transplanted animal culture.

### Step A — Pull the full pick list
- **Inherit verbatim:**
  - Use every tier, not only Primary (RWBEM:226–229).
  - Don't stop at the first result (RWBEM:231–239).
  - "Research the specific place, never the category," as "the specific population, never the species."
- **Change:**
  - **Source file.** A new per-race EBEM Pick List, filled only after Q-4. Nothing pre-exists, so RWBEM:104–105
    ("doesn't require generating new picks") is reversed.
  - **Tier scheme (proposed):**
    - *Primary:* the ruled base population(s).
    - *Secondary:* wild ancestor; feral or introduced populations; other major domestic or wild populations.
    - *Supporting:* closely related taxa, plus captive and lab colonies (always flagged as such).
    - The claim that lower tiers hold more value is a **hypothesis to measure** (HAR-RWBEM-10).
  - **Sub-part rule renamed** (HAR-RWBEM-09).
  - **Pick-selection ban** (HAR-RWBEM-17): never pick for image or setting.

### Step B — Research each pick as a real, specific thing
- **Inherit verbatim:**
  - Inventory first, interpretation later (RWBEM:243–244).
  - Query every pick from more than one angle.
  - Go back to picks already logged as covered (RWBEM:63).
- **Change:**
  - **Inventory categories:** behaviors · signals and their channels · social structure · life history
    (maturation, reproduction, lifespan) · senses and ranges · activity cycle · diet and digestion · habitat
    use and locomotion.
  - **Angles** (HAR-RWBEM-08).
  - **Source standard:** primary ethology and biology literature and scholarly reviews (rule 5). Web summaries
    only as leads.
  - **Terminology:** use the literature's operational definitions. Never import role nouns.
- **Add:**
  - **Revised-model watch.** Record each claim's date and whether the field has since revised it (the
    alpha-wolf case, M-2).
  - **Variation is data.** When two populations disagree, record both. Never average them. The disagreement
    feeds M-1's "which population counts" decision and Q-4.

### Step C — The question, asked per detail
- **Inherit verbatim:**
  - Ask per detail, not per pick (RWBEM:249).
  - Read each detail against the file for what it *is* (RWBEM:274–282).
  - A blocked or unresolved check is not an absence (RWBEM:284–286).
- **Change — the question itself, completely** (HAR-RWBEM-12). Ask "does this race's resolved physiology keep
  the mechanism that produces this?" and check the answer against L1. Never ask "would they have their own
  version?" Proposed read-against map:

  | Step B surfaced a… | Ask it against |
  |---|---|
  | behavior pattern | L1 social instinct and cognition domains; P-3 |
  | signal (any channel) | L1 communication; P-8 (with signal control); P-4 for whether the receiver can perceive it |
  | social-structure trait | L1 social instinct; P-3; P-2 (who raises young) |
  | life-history fact | L1 maturation, reproduction, lifespan; P-2, P-5, P-6 |
  | sensory capacity | L1 senses; P-4 |
  | activity or diet fact | L1; P-1, P-7 |
  | habitat use or locomotion | L1 locomotion; proposed body-plan parameter (`HAR_ULM.md` P-10); P-9 |

- **The answer's outcome uses M-1's tags.** Use Human-dominant, Animal-dominant, Blended, Engineered-novel,
  Suppressed-but-latent, or Unresolved. Never a bare yes or no.

## Notes for the developer
1. **D-2 vs. M-5 needs a ruling.** `MASTER_PLAN.md` M-5 asks for research on the real Alpha Centauri / Proxima
   system. D-2 says these races' worlds and locations are unknown and only vague, generalized terms are
   allowed. Star-system physical facts fit RWBEM's admissible "physical/geographic facts" category
   (RWBEM:143–144). Whether D-2 permits naming a real system at all is your call, and M-5's first link depends
   on it.
2. **RWBEM proper has no role at L1–L3.**
   - For anthros, L3's inherited stock is a *Tepenian* creator culture (fictional, downstream of the Inner
     Tepenia city corpus, `MASTER_PLAN.md` §10), not a real human place, so RWBEM's human-place research doesn't
     feed L3.
   - RWBEM resumes at Phase 7 for any real-world picks that L4 locations get. Its GPS law then applies
     unchanged.
3. **EBEM's sequencing rule depends on M-4.** Domestication history becomes causal only if the creators actually
   used a domesticated population. That makes Q-2 (why and from what the anthros were engineered) a practical
   blocker for EBEM Step B's admissibility, not only for M-4.
4. **The ULM copy of RWBEM wasn't swept line by line here.** It runs 469 lines against the original's 417 and is
   substantially restructured: different section order, a universe-agnostic STANDING PRINCIPLE in place of the
   station-nation law, and the worked example reduced to a pointer. Its human assumptions appear to be the same
   in substance, but a dedicated pass should confirm that before the RWBEM adapter cites it.
5. **Severity convention** is the same as `HAR_CST.md` (Notes item 7).
