# Handwave Register

**Created 2026-10-21 under D-7** (the plausibility standard). It follows the Inner Tepenia precedent,
`InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis/04_Handwave_Register.md`.

**The rule:**
- An entry covers its own mechanism only. **Anything not on this list obeys known biology and physics.**
- Every handwave states what is waved **and what is not**.
- A handwave must stay "within the realm of plausibility." One that no real mechanism could approach is
  "completely fantastical," and is rejected (D-7).

**Codes:** `HW-` for accepted handwaves, `HWC-` for candidates awaiting a ruling. (`C-` is taken by canon.)

**Candidate tags** (from the Inner Tepenia precedent):
- **bend:** biology would have to give if the trait is shown as imagined.
- **cost:** biology holds, but a mechanism, a resource, or a vulnerability must be accepted and shown.
