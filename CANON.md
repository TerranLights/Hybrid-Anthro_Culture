# Canon Intake
**Status:** Intake draft, compiled 2026-10-21. This file RECORDS existing canon from other repos with sources; it does not create canon. The authority is always the source file cited.

## How to read this file
- Each entry has a short code (`C-n`) plus a plain name, then: source `path:line`, an exact short quote or a close paraphrase, and one line on why it matters here.
- Paths are repo-relative, prefixed with the repo name. Folders that are not their own repo are prefixed `media/`. **Shorthand:** `IT-Cities/` = `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations/Cities/`.
- The source's own status is noted where it is weaker than canon (draft, proposed, idea list, in-world belief). "Implied" means the source does not say it outright. Developer rulings D-1 and D-2 (2026-10-21) are recorded in `DECISIONS.md`. Where they conflict with an entry here, they win (see "Conflicts and supersessions").

### Origins and categories

**C-1 · Four Doll representation categories** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Doll_Representation_Categories.md:3-6`. "there are four total representation categories ("Cat.s"). Only one is currently in active use; the other three are established canon, reserved for future characters and projects." The four are Robots, Anthros, Elves, Aliens (`:10-20`). *Relevance:* this is the canon anchor. Anthros, elves, and aliens are confirmed categories. Orcs are not among them.

**C-2 · Every character is a real doll** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Doll_Representation_Categories.md:3`. "Every character in the Dolliverse is based on a real-world, physically-existing doll." *Relevance:* every anthro, elf, or alien character must map to a real doll. That may limit body plans (MASTER_PLAN Q-9).

**C-3 · Categories 2–4 have no characters yet** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Doll_Representation_Categories.md:24-25, 29`. "as of 2026-08-09, every character across every Tepenian Universe project is a Doll represented as a robot (Category 1). Categories 2-4 are confirmed canon but have no characters assigned". *Relevance:* no existing character constrains these races. See conflict X-2, the Shardfall elf doll.

**C-4 · Character intake has no category field** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Doll_Representation_Categories.md:30-33`. The seed-template intake "has no field for which of the four categories a Doll belongs to — it implicitly assumes robot". *Relevance:* the first non-robot character will need that field added (MASTER_PLAN Phase 8).

**C-5 · Inner Tepenia's cities neither place nor exclude these races** — Source: `IT-Cities/City_Development_Passes/Mirny_Subnet/Mirny/04_Phase_09_Populations.md:252`. "Doll Categories 2–4 (Anthros, Elves, Aliens) are canon with no characters assigned … None is sited at Mirny; none is ruled out." The same verdict appears at `IT-Cities/City_Development_Passes/Mirny_Subnet/Zhongshan_Opus/04_Phase_09_Populations.md:103`: "Checked, not assumed". *Relevance:* no Tepenian (Antarctic) population of these races exists in canon, and none is forbidden.

**C-6 · This repo's roster and media assignments** — Source: `Hybrid-Anthro_Culture/README.md:9-31`. This is the developer's own list. Elf: "a cross-dimensional parallel Earth", in *Elven Steel* and *Outer Tepenia: New Centauri*. Orc: parallel Earth, *Elven Steel*. Alien, dog, cat, and rabbit anthros: "the Alpha/Proxima Centauri star system", *New Centauri*. Line 9 calls the roster "genetically-engineered races". *Relevance:* this is the only source anywhere for orcs, and the only source placing anthros and aliens at Centauri. See conflicts X-3, X-4, and X-7.

### Anthros

**C-7 · Anthro origin as first recorded** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Doll_Representation_Categories.md:14-16`. "genetically engineered by humans, by splicing human and animal DNA into human-animal hybrids. Most prominently (but not limited to) "dog-people," "cat-people," and "rabbit-people." **C-8 · Pharmaceutical-lab anthros (idea list, not canon)** — Source: `media/w-other/audio_visual media/full-length TV series/New Antarctica/tv show - prelude books.txt:27`. It turns out, the company had been making genetically-engineered human/animal hybrids (i.e., the ElsaBabe "anthro" dolls)." *Relevance:* this is the earliest anthro concept found. The creator is a human corporation, framed as abusing "innocent lives". It names a real doll line as the anthro basis (Q-9). The era is not stated, and the idea is not canon unless adopted.

### Robots as creators / robot kinship and reproduction (relevant to robot-made anthro lineages)

**C-43 · Robots now build each other** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Doll_Representation_Categories.md:10-11`. "Originally built by humans; later came to be built by each other." *Relevance:* robots already have a self-run creation tradition, the background to D-1's "R" creators.

**C-44 · Fabrication-synthesis chambers and "the Cradle"** — Source: `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md:409-410, 434`. Robots are "physically created via an apparatus". "The Cradle" is the nationwide robot-synthesis infrastructure. "Construction genuinely takes time". *Relevance:* robot creation runs through infrastructure, not individuals. Canon gives no robot capability to create biological life.

**C-45 · The Fundamental Laws are built into how robots make robots** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Laws_of_Robotics.md:3-4, 18-22, 31-35`. Law 1: "A robot will have the full capacity to make decisions to maximize her own future possibilities." The Laws were hard-coded as "a civic/procedural safeguard built into how robots create more robots". *Relevance:* canon does not say whether robots would apply these safeguards to anthros they create. That is a design-charter question for M-4, not an answer.

**C-46 · Minds may only be partly designed** — Source: `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md:422`. A new robot's personality is "only ever partially specced, by law, never fully designed … a fully, explicitly designed mind would leave the resulting robot without a meaningful degree of free will". The historical contrast: Upper Earth humans could fully specify robot personalities. *Relevance:* robot and human creators held opposite norms about designing minds. The bearing on anthro creators is implied only.

**C-47 · Robots keep the human form by choice** — Source: `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md:428`. Robots "see no compelling reason to depart from what evolution already optimized: the human form". *Relevance:* implied bearing on the body plans robot creators might choose. Not stated for anthros.

**C-48 · "Maker/made" is not kinship** — Source: `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md:418`. "a new robot is created by an infrastructure (the Cradle), not a personal "creator" — so kinship built on "who specifically made you" doesn't hold up". *Relevance:* a precedent for how a robot-made lineage might, or might not, relate to its makers.

**C-49 · Robots have no sexual reproduction and no genetic relatedness** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Robot_Universals/2_-_Part_II_Differentiation_Method/08_Reproduction_Kinship_and_the_Absence_of_Genetic_Relatedness/README.md:16-21, 53-56`. "Robots are fabricated through the Cradle network, not born … they do not have genetic relatedness to one another in any sense". *Relevance:* robot creators would be making beings that have the whole kinship and reproduction cluster robots lack (the M-3 reversal).

**C-50 · Robots lack the genetics basis of tribalism** — Source: `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Robot_Universals/3_-_Part_III_Robot_Cultural_Universals/11_Kinship_Clan_and_the_Mentor-Mentee_Institution/README.md:38-42`. L3 stock inherited from robots carries no genetic-kinship model.

**C-51 · Robots live for centuries** — Source: `IT-Cities/City_Vision_Notes/Esperanza.md:40`. "Robots can live for centuries … making robots, not human genealogical lines, the primary carriers of continuous lived memory". *Relevance:* robot creators could outlive many anthro generations, which matters for M-4 drift and M-7.

### Human reproduction and biology precedents

**C-52 · Artificial wombs are normal in Tepenia** — Source: `IT-Cities/City_Vision_Notes/Esperanza.md:25, 37-38`. "Children are predominantly born via artificial womb technology". **C-56 · Gestation in low or spun gravity is untested (proposed)** — Source: `InnerTepeniaGDD/Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Object_Oriented_Location_Synthesis/04_Handwave_Register.md:52`.
