# Ethological Pick List — Rabbit-People (M-2 Step A)

**Status:** `[Proposed]` draft, 2026-10-21. D-4 fixes the base **species** only. Under M-2's sequencing rule,
until a lineage charter says which population its creators used, only **mechanisms** are admissible. The
husbandry and domestication history of any population below is a coordinate, never a cause.

| Tier | Pick | Management status | Why picked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Wild *O. cuniculus* in its native range (Iberian Peninsula and southern France), including both recognized subspecies | wild | The base species in its own ecology. This is the reference for unmodified mechanisms |
| Secondary | Introduced wild and feral populations with long-term field studies (e.g. Australia, Great Britain, New Zealand) | feral / wild | Most of the classic field literature on warren social structure comes from introduced populations. The variation against the native range is data (M-2 Step B) |
| Secondary | Domestic rabbits (pet, meat, and fancy breeds) | domestic | Shows what domestication changed. It becomes causal only if a lineage charter names domestic stock |
| Supporting | Laboratory rabbit colonies | lab (always flagged) | Physiology, sensory, and reproductive data unavailable from the wild. Captive conditions are flagged on every claim |
| Supporting | Other leporids (*Lepus* hares, *Sylvilagus* cottontails) | wild | Contrast only. These are different genera with different rearing and sociality. Never a substitute for *O. cuniculus* data |

Researchers add picks in their own logs under "Additional picks proposed." Picks are merged here at review.
